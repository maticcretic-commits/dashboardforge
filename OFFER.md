**Management dashboards for finance teams — built from your existing Excel/CSV/ERP data in 5–10 days.**
Automate recurring MIS and make key KPIs visible, without replacing existing systems.

## The problem we remove

Your team spends hours every month consolidating spreadsheets into the MIS. Numbers arrive late, versions disagree, and nobody trusts the file. We replace that with a decision-ready dashboard covering agreed KPIs — revenue, expenses, collections, profitability, ageing, variance, branch/product performance — refreshed from your data, not rebuilt by hand.

## Three fixed packages

**Focus — ₹15,000, 5 days.** One focused dashboard, 5–8 decision KPIs, from your existing data. The low-risk entry: prove the value on one reporting pain before committing further.

**Build — ₹35,000, 10 days.** Multi-view management dashboard plus one agreed automation/reporting workflow (e.g. the monthly pack builds itself). The core offer for teams that live in their MIS.

**Partner — ₹8,000/month.** Defined maintenance so the dashboard stays live: one data-source update cycle per month, fixes, and one agreed improvement request per month. Explicit limits — this is a maintenance plan, not unlimited support.

Every package page states exactly what is included and excluded. Fixed scope, fixed price, fixed date. If your data needs cleaning beyond the agreed scope, you will know the cost before we start — never after.
